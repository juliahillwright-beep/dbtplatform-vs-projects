dbt platform (Enterprise) vs. Snowflake dbt Projects

Build pipelines faster. Ship data products sooner.

dbt platform gives teams the managed infrastructure, governed collaboration, and tooling to build data pipelines quickly and ship reliable data products faster. Snowflake dbt Projects is a solid starting point for small, Snowflake-only teams that already know dbt well — but it's a place to author and run dbt, not a managed platform. CI/CD, retry, alerting, observability, version upgrades, and cross-project governance are things your engineers build and keep running.

Why it matters

Snowflake dbt Projects runs the compute. dbt platform (Enterprise) runs the platform — the trust, governance, and context layer your data stack needs as complexity grows.

🧰

Free to run ≠ free to operate

Snowflake dbt Projects has no license fee, but someone on your team builds and maintains the CI/CD pipeline, failure recovery, alerting, job history, and dbt version upgrades — and that assumes existing dbt expertise to begin with. dbt platform replaces that build cost rather than adding to it, and ships with onboarding from the team that builds dbt.

🏗️

Multi-team collaboration

dbt Mesh enables cross-project references, contracts, and access controls — so teams build on trusted upstream assets instead of recreating logic.

⚡

Development efficiency

The dbt v2 engine gives developers live diagnostics and column autocomplete in Studio and VS Code. Snowflake Workspaces runs v2 as an execution runtime only — no language server, so errors surface after the run finishes, not as you type.

📐

Trusted metrics — fully portable

Semantic Layer powered by MetricFlow centralizes metric definitions across all teams and all warehouses, versioned and tested alongside your models. Because MetricFlow is warehouse-agnostic, your metric definitions move with you — every dashboard, report, and AI agent (via MCP) uses the same definition of revenue regardless of which data platform you're on. Snowflake Semantic Views are Snowflake-only and managed as database objects outside your project's version control and CI.

🔍

Lineage & impact analysis

Column-level, filterable lineage across projects — plus impact analysis in the editor — lets you understand downstream impact before making changes. Snowflake's Horizon lineage only covers materialized objects after a first run, caps the DAG view at 300 models, and stops at the project boundary.

🔒

Governance controls

Model-level access modifiers (public/protected/private), environment separation, PR-based approvals, and audit history meet production standards and compliance requirements. In Snowflake dbt Projects, governance is all-or-nothing at the project-object level, and a developer with prod grants can build straight to prod from a workspace.

💰

Cost efficiency

dbt State skips unchanged models on every run, and Cost Insights proves what it saved — per model and per job. On Snowflake, every run also holds a user-managed warehouse through parse and compile (no serverless tasks for dbt), and there's no view to show finance what State actually saved.

🎨

Accessible to non-technical builders

dbt Canvas gives analysts and BI developers a drag-and-drop visual interface to build in dbt natively — no SQL required — and every Canvas model compiles to version-controlled dbt SQL. Snowflake dbt Projects requires engineering experience to use; there is no equivalent visual development layer available in the tool.

Find your fit

Which is right for your team?

Both options serve real needs — it comes down to where you are on the maturity curve today, how much dbt expertise and engineering capacity you can commit to running it, and where you need to be in 12–18 months.

Choose dbt platform (Enterprise) when you need to:

The right fit for teams operating dbt as a production data capability.

  • Keep engineers on data products instead of building and maintaining CI/CD, orchestration, and observability tooling
  • Onboard analysts and newer dbt users safely — Canvas, guided onboarding, and live diagnostics
  • Scale dbt across multiple teams and domains
  • Standardize and govern shared business logic
  • Improve deployment reliability and developer productivity
  • Reduce warehouse costs through more efficient execution
  • Meet security, compliance, and audit requirements
  • Maintain flexibility as data strategies evolve
  • Enable automation and simplicity at scale

Snowflake dbt Projects is a good fit when:

A solid starting point for small, Snowflake-only teams that already know dbt and can staff the maintenance themselves.

  • Your team has deep prior dbt experience — Snowflake dbt Projects assumes it, with no guided onboarding and no in-editor diagnostics to catch mistakes
  • You can dedicate engineering headcount to platform maintenance — building and owning CI/CD, retry, alerting, job history, and dbt upgrades yourself (typically 2–6 months of senior engineering time to build, plus ongoing upkeep)
  • You're fully committed to Snowflake with no multi-warehouse plans — and no M&A on the horizon
  • You have a small, single-team project (fewer than 5 developers) with no cross-team model sharing
  • You're running straightforward transformations in test or dev — not yet production-ready, and without production SLAs that need automatic retry or model-level alerting
  • No analysts or non-engineers need to contribute — there's no visual development layer
Snowflake dbt Projects is a solid place to start if you already know dbt and can staff the tooling. dbt platform is built for organizations that need to operate dbt as a reliable, governed capability across teams, domains, and data infrastructure — without dedicating engineers to run it.
