Why it matters

The platform that makes AI, analytics,
and self-service reliable at scale

dbt platform (Enterprise) is the trust, governance, and context layer your data stack needs as complexity grows.

🏗️

Multi-team collaboration

dbt Mesh enables cross-project references, contracts, and access controls — so teams build on trusted upstream assets instead of recreating logic.

⚡

Development efficiency

Fusion engine with enhanced diagnostics + dbt State means only changed models are built. Customers report 25–35% fewer models built and 15% lower Snowflake compute costs.

📐

Trusted metrics — fully portable

Semantic Layer powered by MetricFlow centralizes metric definitions across all teams and all warehouses. Because MetricFlow is warehouse-agnostic, your metric definitions move with you — every dashboard, report, and AI output uses the same definition of revenue regardless of which data platform you're on. Snowflake Semantic Views lock you to Snowflake.

🔍

Lineage & impact analysis

Column-level, filterable lineage across projects lets you understand downstream impact before making changes — not after they've broken production.

🔒

Governance controls

Role-based access, approval workflows, and audit history meet production standards and compliance requirements that Snowflake dbt Projects' limited deployment controls cannot.

💰

Cost efficiency

State-Aware Orchestration skips unchanged models on every run. Snowflake dbt Projects executes the full DAG every time — burning compute on work that doesn't need doing.

🎨

Accessible to non-technical builders

dbt Canvas gives analysts and BI developers a drag-and-drop visual interface to build in dbt natively — no SQL required. Snowflake dbt Projects requires engineering experience to use; there is no equivalent visual development layer available in the tool.

Find your fit

Which is right for your team?

Both options serve real needs — it comes down to where you are today and where you need to go.

Choose dbt platform (Enterprise) when you need to:

The right fit for teams operating dbt as a production data capability.

  • Scale dbt across multiple teams and domains
  • Standardize and govern shared business logic
  • Improve deployment reliability and developer productivity
  • Reduce warehouse costs through more efficient execution
  • Meet security, compliance, and audit requirements
  • Maintain flexibility as data strategies evolve
  • Enable automation and simplicity at scale

Snowflake dbt Projects is a good fit when:

A reasonable starting point for smaller engineering teams on Snowflake.

  • Your team has extensive prior experience with dbt and is comfortable dedicating engineering headcount for platform maintenance away from product development
  • Your team is small (under 3 people) and working on a single project
  • You have no requirement for governance, access controls, or audit trails
  • Snowflake is your only data platform and you don't anticipate that changing
  • Scale, cross-team collaboration, and enterprise deployment are not current priorities
dbt Platform is built for organizations that need to operate dbt as a reliable, governed capability across teams, domains, and data infrastructure.
